## 📥 Vulnerability Ingestion Pipeline

If you discover a security exploit, edge-case leak, or script vulnerability within this repository, **do not open a public issue or pull request**. Publicly broadcasting an exploit puts live systems at risk. Instead, route your findings through our secure ingestion channel:

1. **Compile a Report:** Document the bug with a clear Proof of Concept (PoC) or steps to reproduce the issue.
2. **Direct Transmission:** Send your encrypted breakdown directly to the master ingestion inbox:
   📩 **mailto:rayyan.workhost@gmail.com**
